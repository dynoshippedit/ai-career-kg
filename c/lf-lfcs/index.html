The honest cost
Cost$445
$445 for the exam alone. A bundle with the LFS207 course is $645; a THRIVE-ONE subscription bundle is a third option.
Validity2 years — stated on the official page.
RenewalRe-certify before expiry. The purchase includes 12 months of exam eligibility, two exam attempts and two simulator attempts.
AssessmentOnline, proctored, PERFORMANCE-BASED exam — 2 hours doing real tasks, not multiple choice
ProctoredYes

The Linux Foundation's system-administration credential, built from community-identified competencies.

Read this before you buy

PERFORMANCE-BASED, which is why it is worth more than its price suggests: you do real tasks in a live environment for two hours rather than answering questions. Note the neighbouring LFCE exam was RETIRED in May 2022 — if you find it recommended anywhere, that advice is at least four years stale.
