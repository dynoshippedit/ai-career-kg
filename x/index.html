How much we actually checked

Not every entry is verified to the same standard, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. Of 889 entries: 859 carry the date we last checked the link ourselves; 30 were added by the operator and are marked operator-added rather than given a date we did not earn; 68 sit behind a bot wall or render only in a browser, so our checker could not read them — kept because a human decided they are real, not because a script proved it; 12 are flagged needs checking in the table below and have not been through a final review.

Nothing here is padding — every row was reviewed by a person before it shipped. But "reviewed" and "machine-verified today" are different things, and the Verified column tells you which one you are looking at.
