Families

IT, Systems & Cloud

The most reliable no-degree entry into tech. You start by fixing things for people, and the work compounds into systems, networking and cloud.

Cybersecurity

Defensive security work — watching, investigating and responding. Most people arrive here from help desk or networking rather than straight off the street.

AI / Machine Learning

Building and shipping models and the systems around them. The most hyped family on this site, and the one where we are most careful to be honest about entry difficulty.

UX & Product Design

Deciding how a product should work for the people using it. The most portfolio-driven family here, and the most genuinely open to people without a degree.

Software Engineering

Building the software itself — front end, back end, mobile, and the testing and delivery around it.

Data

Turning data into answers people act on — analysis, reporting, and the modelling underneath it.
