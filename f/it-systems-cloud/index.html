IT, Systems & Cloud

The most reliable no-degree entry into tech. You start by fixing things for people, and the work compounds into systems, networking and cloud.

Entry point

Best first stop if you have no experience and want to be employable fastest.

Roles, easiest entry first

Cloud Engineer

You design and run the infrastructure that applications live on, in code rather than by hand.

✓No degree needed

Cloud Support

You keep cloud infrastructure running and help engineers and customers when it misbehaves.

✓No degree needed

FinOps / Cloud Cost Engineer

You find out why the cloud bill doubled, and you make it stop.

✓No degree needed

Help Desk / IT Support

You are the person who fixes it when someone's computer, login or printer stops working.

✓No degree needed

Network Administrator

You keep the network up — switches, routing, wifi, VPNs, and the mystery of why one floor can't print.

✓No degree needed

Platform Engineer

You build the paved road other engineers ship on, so they stop reinventing the same pipeline.

✓No degree needed

Solutions Architect

You decide how the pieces fit together, and you have to defend the decision to people paying for it.

✓No degree needed

Systems Administrator

You keep the servers, accounts and services running that everyone else quietly depends on.

✓No degree needed
