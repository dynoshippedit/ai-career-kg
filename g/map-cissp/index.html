CISSP knowledge map

Every objective of the CISSP exam outline (effective April 15, 2024), 1.1 to 8.5, pointing to the lesson that teaches it — or logged as a gap where no lesson exists yet. Objectives are numbered and paraphrased in the guidebook’s own words; the outline text itself is never reproduced.

Published 2026-10-09 · generated from the lesson data · 17 of 62 objectives have a lesson

The issuer’s rule, stated once

Full CISSP certification needs five cumulative years of work experience in two or more of the eight domains — a degree or an approved credential can stand in for one year. Passing the exam without the experience makes someone an Associate of ISC2. The lessons teach the bodies of knowledge; they do not confer the certification.

AI in the outline

ISC2 weaves AI-specific security tasks across all eight domains rather than numbering separate AI objectives, so the map treats AI as context inside each domain’s lessons — cross-linked to the AI security track — not as standalone entries.
