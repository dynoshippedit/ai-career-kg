CAISP knowledge map

The seven chapters of the CAISP syllabus (Practical DevSecOps), each pointing to the guidebook lessons that teach it. Chapter titles are the issuer’s; everything else is the guidebook’s own words.

Published 2026-10-09 · generated from the lesson data · 7 of 7 chapters have a lesson

Two credentials, never merged

CAISP (Practical DevSecOps) is a separate credential from CertNexus CAIP, which the site lists separately. This map covers CAISP only.

Edition note

The lessons teach the OWASP LLM Top 10 2026 edition (verified against the official OWASP GenAI Security Project repo, 2026-10-09). The project brief named the 2025 edition — that reference is pending the owner’s confirmation.
