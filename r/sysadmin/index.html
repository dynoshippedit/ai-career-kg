Systems Administrator

You keep the servers, accounts and services running that everyone else quietly depends on.

✓No degree needed ◆Moderate — a clear step up from help desk, and a well-worn one.

Can I actually do this?

Rarely a first job, commonly a second. The usual route is help desk first, then Linux depth and internal moves. Among the no-degree steps available once you are already in IT it has a low barrier — that is our reasoning from the prerequisites, not a hiring source we have checked.

Who it suits. People who would rather understand a system than memorise its buttons, and who are comfortable being responsible when it breaks.

Runway. Realistic within months of help desk experience plus deliberate Linux practice.

Coming from another job?

Already on a help desk? This is the natural next step, and your troubleshooting history counts as real experience. Help Desk / IT Support

The route

Four stations, in order. Each one is a thing you finish before the next matters.

  2. 2 Station two

    Attest strategically

    LFCS is the one worth considering, and specifically because it is performance-based — two hours doing real tasks rather than answering questions, which is a stronger signal than most paid multiple-choice exams. At $445 it is not cheap, so earn it when you can also show a lab. AVOID LFCE: that exam was retired in May 2022 and any advice recommending it is at least four years stale.

  3. 3 Station three

    Prove it

    A certificate says you passed a test. These say you can do the job.

    A server you rebuilt from nothing

    Provision a Linux server, harden it, run a real service on it, then destroy and rebuild it from your own notes. The notes are the artifact.

    An automated fix

    Find a task you did twice by hand, script it, and document what it now saves. Sysadmin work is judged on what stops needing you.

    A documented incident

    Break something on purpose, diagnose it, and write it up as an incident report — symptom, investigation, root cause, fix.

  4. 4 Station four

    Get hired

    Search these exact titles

    • systems administrator
    • Linux administrator
    • systems engineer
    • infrastructure administrator

    Who hires for this. Managed service providers, in-house IT teams, hosting companies, universities, and anywhere with servers of its own.

    Rebuild notes and an incident write-up show judgement under pressure in a way a certificate cannot, because they record what you did when something was actually wrong. That is our reasoning about what is checkable, not a hiring statistic we have verified.

    On salary

    We don't publish salary estimates. Numbers copied between blogs drift from reality, and a wrong number costs you real negotiating power. When we have a verified public source, it goes here with its date.
