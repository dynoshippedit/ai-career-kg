Cloud Engineer

You design and run the infrastructure that applications live on, in code rather than by hand.

✓No degree needed ◆Harder — this is a mid-tier role and we are not going to pretend it is an entry point.

Can I actually do this?

Not a first job. The honest route is help desk or cloud support first, then this. Direct entry happens with strong project evidence, not with a certificate alone.

Who it suits. People who would rather automate a task than repeat it, and who are comfortable being responsible for things that cost money while they sleep.

Runway. Realistic after cloud support or sysadmin experience plus an Associate-tier certificate and real project work.

Coming from another job?

Coming from cloud support or networking? That is the expected path — this role is a promotion, not a career change. Cloud Support Network Administrator

Also advertised as

  • Cloud Infrastructure Engineer
  • Platform Engineer
  • DevOps Engineer (cloud-leaning)
  • Site Reliability Engineer (junior)

  3. 3 Station three

    Prove it

    A certificate says you passed a test. These say you can do the job.

    Everything in code

    Rebuild a working environment entirely from Terraform or Bicep — network, compute, storage, permissions — then destroy and recreate it from scratch to prove it is reproducible.

    A pipeline that actually deploys

    Wire a CI pipeline that tests and deploys on merge, with a documented rollback you have really executed at least once.

    A cost and failure write-up

    Document what your environment costs per month and what happens when a component dies. Engineers who think about the bill and the blast radius are rarer than engineers who can deploy.
