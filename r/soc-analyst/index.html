Cybersecurity

SOC Analyst

You watch the alerts, work out which ones matter, and investigate the ones that do.

✓No degree needed ◆Medium — harder as a direct entry than help desk, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Can I actually do this?

Realistic without a degree, but usually reached via IT support or networking rather than as a first job. Direct entry happens with Security+ and genuine lab evidence.

Who it suits. People who enjoy investigation: noticing the odd detail, chasing it down, and writing up what happened clearly enough that someone else can act on it.

Runway. Realistic with Security+ plus documented home-lab investigation work.

Coming from another job?

Already working help desk or in networking? Those are the two confirmed feeder roles — you are partway in already. Help Desk / IT Support

  3. 3 Station three

    Prove it

    A certificate says you passed a test. These say you can do the job.

    Home SOC lab

    Run Sysmon with a free-tier Splunk or an Elastic stack, simulate attacks against it, and produce three documented investigation write-ups. Note: the commonly recommended 'Splunk Fundamentals 1/2/3' was retired — use Splunk's current learning paths instead.

    https://www.splunk.com/en_us/training/learning-paths.html

    LetsDefend investigation reports

    Keep your free-tier investigations as portfolio artifacts. Real alert triage you can talk through in an interview.

    Blue-team CTF write-ups

    Public, dated write-ups of defensive challenges.
